A note before you board

NON-PLACE is a concept album set on one long flight home across timezones. A non-place, a term from anthropologist Marc Augé, is a transient space where you feel detached, anonymous, and strangely familiar all at once. The album follows the flight from boarding to landing, day to night, while I try to hold onto the memories of the city I left as they fade like a dream. Each song is a persona that only exists at one point in the trip.

I produced the music, and built this interactive album experience website as the seatback screen in front of you. A plane flies around the 3D globe from Pittsburgh to Seoul in sync with the tracks, with a cabin announcement before each one.
